How we keep it fair
  • Counting starts after a steady walking rhythm, so bumps and taps don't count.
  • Shaking, vibration and machine-perfect rhythms are detected and ignored.
  • Everything runs on your phone. No GPS, no location, and your motion data never leaves the device.

    How mining works
    • Mining runs on Acki Nacki's Bee Engine. It rewards real activity, and your walking is the activity.
    • NACKL goes straight to your own Acki Nacki Wallet. TalonHive never holds your coins.
    • Your wallet must have ecosystem participation turned on (Acki Nacki asks for this once, in the wallet).
    • Reward amounts are set by Bee Engine, not by TalonHive.
    • Keep the app open while mining: sessions are computed on your phone and the screen stays on.
    • Only step counts go to the chain as mining taps. Raw motion and location never leave your phone.
    TalonHive will never ask for your seed phrase or keys. Mining uses separate Mining Keys that you can revoke in your wallet at any time.
