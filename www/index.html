Hold the phone or keep it in a pocket. Keep the app open: the screen stays on during a walk (a phone limit for web apps; the Android app will count with the screen off).

How we keep it fair
  • Counting starts after a steady walking rhythm, so bumps and taps don't count.
  • Shaking, vibration and machine-perfect rhythms are detected and ignored.
  • Everything runs on your phone. No GPS, no location, and your motion data never leaves the device.
MiningComing soon

Each verified step will power NACKL mining through Acki Nacki's Bee Engine, after you connect your Acki Nacki Wallet and allow mining. That switches on once Acki Nacki issues our app id. For now, walks are counted but not yet rewarded.

TalonHive will never ask for your seed phrase or keys. Mining uses separate Mining Keys that you can revoke in your wallet at any time.
